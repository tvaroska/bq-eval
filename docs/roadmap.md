# Roadmap (`bq-eval`)

## Where we are

`bq-eval` `v0.3.0` (`M7`) delivers a zero-debt (`111` tests, `mypy --strict`, `ast-grep` layered `Rule 0` firewall, `standards_debt.json = 0`) Dual-Path BigQuery SQL and offline SQLite evaluation and FinOps engine over Light Capture (`BQAA` `agent_events`) and Full Capture (`OTel` `_AllSpans`), backed by a `.stitch/DESIGN.md`-governed 4-tab GM3 Light workbench (`bq-eval serve`), committed `343`-run replay receipts (`benchmarks/results/m2_live_receipt.json` + SHA-256 manifest), verified `capacity` and `docubridge` wire contracts, and layered `bq_eval/{contract,engine,showcase,cli}/` subpackages. `L1`, `L2`, and `L3` are locked as-is (`I-Score: 62/100 B`, `4/6` Stop & Read). Two `L4` milestones remain to clear Wave 2 deep-authority defects (`S-Score: 54/100 C -> Q1`) and ship cold bootstrap: `M8` (`v0.4.0`: security, dual-path/SQLite parity, executable Day-2 judge quota guard, receipt reconciliation, and single-project Terraform/bootstrap held ready locally) and `M9` (`v0.5.0`: subtractive Dark LOC cleanup and dynamic `--scenario-file` FinOps computation).
