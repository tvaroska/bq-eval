# Roadmap (`bq-eval`)

## Where we are

`bq-eval` `v0.3.0` (`M7`) delivers an offline-verified (`111` tests, `mypy --strict`, `ast-grep` layered `Rule 0` firewall, `standards_debt.json = 0`) Dual-Path BigQuery SQL and SQLite evaluation and FinOps engine over Light Capture (`BQAA` `agent_events`) and Full Capture (`OTel` `_AllSpans`), backed by a [`.stitch/DESIGN.md`](../.stitch/DESIGN.md)-governed 4-tab GM3 Light workbench (`bq-eval serve`), committed replay bundle ([`benchmarks/results/m2_live_receipt.json`](../benchmarks/results/m2_live_receipt.json) + SHA-256 manifest), `capacity` & `docubridge` wire contracts, and layered `bq_eval/{contract,engine,showcase,cli}/` subpackages. `L1` and `L2` are locked (`I-Score: 62/100 B`, `4/6` Stop & Read; `S-Score: 54/100 C`), while `L3` [`README.md`](../README.md) unlocks in `M8` alongside receipt reconciliation so doc-contract truth, cold-start onboarding, and cross-doc contradictions align before hardening (`M9`), live BigQuery bootstrap + empirical proof (`M10`), and the portable Enterprise Multi-Cloud North Star (`M11`).
