# Roadmap (`bq-eval`)

## Where we are

`bq-eval` `v0.3.0` (`M7`, shipped) plus `M8` and `M9` (**implemented locally, pending `v0.4.0` / `v0.5.0` release**) deliver an offline-verified (`135` tests, `mypy --strict`, `ast-grep` folder-level `Rule 0` firewall, `standards_debt.json = 0`) Dual-Path BigQuery SQL and SQLite evaluation and FinOps engine over Light Capture (`BQAA` `agent_events`) and Full Capture (`OTel` `_AllSpans`), backed by a [`.stitch/DESIGN.md`](../.stitch/DESIGN.md)-governed 4-tab GM3 Light workbench (`bq-eval serve` with strict SHA-256 CSP and loopback guard), reconciled replay bundle ([`benchmarks/results/m2_live_receipt.json`](../benchmarks/results/m2_live_receipt.json) + SHA-256 manifest), executable per-app Day-2 quota guard procedure, `EvalBackend` `Protocol`, `bq-eval bqaa` CLI ingestion, dynamic `--scenario-file` FinOps, and layered `bq_eval/{contract,engine,showcase,cli}/` subpackages. Next planned milestones are live BigQuery bootstrap + empirical proof (`M10`) and the portable Enterprise Multi-Cloud North Star (`M11`).
